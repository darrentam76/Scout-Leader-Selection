# Knowledge Base & FAQ Reference

### 1. App Purpose
- **Goal:** Collect leader profiles (experience, expertise, partner preferences, event availability) to optimize IC assignment across 25 annual troop activities using an automated algorithm.
- **Rules:** Ensures every event has at least one Senior Leader (>= 3 years experience) paired with Junior Leaders ("舊帶新"), while capping workload at 2-3 IC slots per leader.

### 2. Data Protection & Security Policy
- **Data Encryption:** All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.3) and stored securely in localized databases.
- **AI Model Privacy:** API requests to the optimization engine use zero-data retention endpoints; data is NEVER used for training third-party AI models.
- **Access Control:** Only authorized Troop Scouters can access final deployment results.
- **Retention:** Leader profiles are archived securely at the end of the scouting year and deleted upon request.
